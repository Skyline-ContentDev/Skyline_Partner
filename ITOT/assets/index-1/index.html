Does the customer use Cisco ISE?

Lead with addition, not replacement. Cyber Vision’s new native network segmentation complements Cisco ISE and Cisco Secure Firewall rather than replacing them. Customers can use Cyber Vision’s segmentation capability for east-west segmentation within the OT network. ISE keeps handling identity-based policies, and Secure Firewall keeps covering the boundary between IT and OT. This protects what the customer already invested in while growing the footprint and capabilities.
